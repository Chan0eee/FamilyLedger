Privacy overview

Information FamilyLedger uses

Depending on the features you choose, FamilyLedger processes profile information, receipt images and extracted purchase details, manually entered records, linked-bank balances and transactions, and eligible purchase emails.

How information is used

This information is used to organize spending, calculate summaries, prevent duplicate counting, show family-level activity, and operate connected features. FamilyLedger does not sell personal financial information.

Your choices

You can disconnect bank and email connections from the app. Household members share spending records needed for the family view, while each person’s linked-account connection details remain private to them.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact familyledger.us.support@gmail.com for privacy questions, account deletion, or data access requests.

Bank connection availability

Bank connections are provided through Plaid and depend on institution support and institution-specific availability. Some banks may be temporarily unavailable during a migration or maintenance window. Receipt and manual entry features remain available without a bank connection.
