1. Description of the Service

FamilyLedger is a household expense-tracking application that helps individuals and families organize spending in one place. Depending on your plan tier, features of the Service include:

  • Capturing or uploading receipt photos and extracting merchant, line-item, and total information using automated AI processing.
  • Entering manual expense records and tracking cash on hand.
  • Linking eligible bank and credit card accounts via Plaid Inc. to import balances and transactions automatically.
  • Connecting a Gmail account (read-only access) to import eligible purchase and order-confirmation receipts.
  • Automatic deduplication between receipt records and bank transactions, monthly summaries, calendar views, category analytics, and shared family ledgers.

Informational Tool Only: FamilyLedger is an expense-organization tool. We are not a bank, financial institution, certified public accountant, financial advisor, or tax advisor. Nothing in the Service constitutes financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.

3. Family Sharing and Household Groups

  • Every verified user starts with a personal family ledger as its owner. Owners on an eligible Pro plan may generate invite codes to invite up to five (5) total household members.
  • When you join a family group, your expense records (receipts, manual entries, and imported bank transactions) are shared with and visible to all members of that family group. Your personal connection credentials and linked account identifiers (such as which specific bank or Gmail address you linked) remain private to you.
  • The family owner manages the family group's subscription tier and may remove a member from the group. If a member is removed or joins another family group, that member's own expense records and linked connections move with them to their new solo or target family ledger.
